02 / The brief

A local site has four jobs. Most have none of them covered.

We start every project by checking these four, on your current site, on a phone, on cell data. That check is part of the free estimate.

01

Show up in the map pack

Most local searches never scroll past the three map results. That placement comes from your Google Business Profile, consistent listings, reviews, and location-relevant pages — not from a prettier homepage.

02

Make the next step obvious

Tap-to-call in the header, a quote form that is three fields not twelve, hours and directions where a person on a phone expects them. If someone has to hunt, they go back to the results page.

03

Prove you are real

Your own project photos, your license and insurance numbers, reviews pulled from Google, the neighborhoods you actually cover. Stock images of somebody else's kitchen do the opposite.

04

Load fast on a phone

Your customer is in a parking lot on two bars of LTE. A site that takes six seconds to paint has already lost them. We build lean and measure it before launch, not after.

05 / Common questions

Before you reach out.

What does a website actually cost?

It depends on page count, whether you need booking or e-commerce, and how much of the copy and photography you already have. That is why the estimate is free — you get a real number for your job instead of a range that means nothing. Most local service-business sites land in a predictable band, and we will tell you where yours sits on the first call.

Do I have to sign up for SEO to get a website?

No. Plenty of clients take the site, take the keys, and run it themselves. The build is complete on its own — Search Console, analytics, and Business Profile are set up either way. SEO is a separate engagement you can start whenever it makes sense.

Who owns the site when it is done?

You do. The domain stays in your name, the code and content are yours, and you get the logins. We do not hold a site hostage to a monthly fee.

How long does a build take?

Typically three to six weeks from the signed scope. The variable is almost always content — photos, service descriptions, and staff bios. Projects where we handle the copy move faster than ones waiting on a busy owner to send text.

Do you work with businesses outside South Florida?

Our focus is Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach, because local SEO rewards knowing the market. We take work outside the tri-county area case by case — ask.

My site is only two years old. Is a rebuild overkill?

Sometimes, yes. If the bones are good, we will tell you that and quote the fixes instead — speed, mobile layout, forms, and search. We would rather do the smaller job well than sell you a rebuild you did not need.
