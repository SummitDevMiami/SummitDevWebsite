Design & SEO — Hallandale, FL

The Kitchen Remodeling Co.

Kitchen remodeling in Miami is one of the most competitive local search markets there is — every established shop in the county is bidding for the same handful of terms. We designed and built the site, then took on search.

Inside the first month it went from zero clicks a month in Google Search to roughly 1,400, and started landing on page one for several of the keywords that actually bring in remodel jobs.

K Construction & Development Corp.

The parent company to The Kitchen Remodeling Co. — luxury remodels and ground-up custom homes, building in South Florida since 2005. We handled design and build.

Because the two are sister companies, the brief was to make the sites read as family without making them twins: the same typographic system and dark editorial hero, restructured around a much broader scope of work — concrete restoration and custom homes rather than kitchens alone.
