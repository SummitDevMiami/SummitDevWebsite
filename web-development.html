Project types

Most of our work falls into five shapes.

01

Service business site

The common one. Home, services, service areas, about, reviews, contact. Built for contractors, HVAC, plumbing, roofing, landscaping, cleaning, auto, legal, and medical practices. Five to twelve pages, usually.

02

Multi-location business

A page per location with its own hours, phone, map, staff, and reviews — structured so each one can rank in its own city instead of competing with the others.

03

Booking or scheduling

Salons, clinics, studios, and trades that book by appointment. We integrate the scheduler you already use, or set one up, so bookings land in your calendar and not in a voicemail queue.

04

Small storefront

A focused product catalog with real checkout, shipping, and tax — for shops with a manageable catalog, not a 5,000-SKU warehouse.

05

Rebuild or rescue

You have a site, but it is slow, unmanageable, or you have lost access to it. We audit what is there, tell you honestly whether it needs a rebuild or just repairs, and quote whichever is right.

How it is built

Built to be fast first, and editable second.

Most local business sites do not need a heavy content management system running behind them. We build lean by default and add a CMS when there is a real reason — a blog you will actually write, a menu that changes weekly, a team page you update.

If you are already on WordPress and happy with it, we will work in WordPress. The point is that you can maintain the thing after we hand it over, not that we get to use our favorite tool.

Decisions we make deliberately

  • No page builder bloat. Drag-and-drop builders ship enormous amounts of unused code. They are why so many local sites take eight seconds to load.
  • Your photos over stock. Real trucks, real crews, real storefronts. If you do not have usable photos, we will tell you what to shoot on a phone.
  • Copy that names the work. "Emergency AC repair in Hollywood, 7 days a week" beats "Excellence in comfort solutions."
  • Hosting you control. Set up in your own account so you are never locked out of your own website.
